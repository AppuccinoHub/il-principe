Learn

Real training help, from people who know dogs. Positive training only: treats, patience, no yelling.

The Royal Training Academy

Three rules: 3 to 5 minutes, tiny treats, one person talking at a time. Take turns.

  1. Name game. Say "Nico!" and treat the second he looks at you.
  2. Sit. Hold a treat over his nose and move it back. Treat when his bottom lands.
  3. Come. Crouch down, say "Come!" in a happy voice. Treat when he arrives.
  4. Touch. Hold out a hand. Treat when his nose touches it.
